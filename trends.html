Why can ARGUS show zero trends after analysing thousands of teams?
Because “analysed” does not mean “verified.” ARGUS requires the exact latest 10 played matches with calendar continuity through yesterday. If the recent overlay or required match statistics are incomplete, the system deliberately returns zero rather than infer, backfill or fabricate a streak.
How trends affect betting decisions
Trend evidence can support an existing thesis, but by policy it cannot create PRIME on its own. It is descriptive historical evidence and must remain subordinate to current price, broader model evidence, data quality and governance checks.
