What CLEAR actually means
CLEAR means the Shield found no blocking issue among the decisions it actually reviewed. If zero matches were reviewed, CLEAR is not a green light to bet; it simply means there was no active decision for the Shield to evaluate.
Why recent losses appear here
Recent official misses are model records, not user profit or loss. They are used as caution evidence so ARGUS can reduce urgency after errors instead of chasing them with larger stakes or more bets.
