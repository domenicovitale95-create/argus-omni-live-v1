Why WATCH is not the same as VALUE
WATCH can reflect a strong model probability when history quality is sufficient, even without a validated market price advantage. VALUE and PRIME require a real price and a positive edge after the training-memory adjustment. The interface keeps those states separate so a probability is never mistaken for a betting recommendation.
