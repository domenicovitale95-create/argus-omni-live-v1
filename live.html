How ARGUS decides whether to act live
ARGUS combines current price, model probability, live pressure, momentum, data quality, market freshness and hard vetoes. A recommendation disappears as soon as the evidence becomes stale, contradictory or too weak. “Signal strength” is a combined decision score, not a guaranteed win probability.
What the live labels mean
LIVE PRIME is the highest live-conviction tier. STRONG means several independent signals align. WATCH means the setup deserves attention but still lacks enough confirmation for a stronger action. No valid setup means no play.
