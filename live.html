How ARGUS calculates live probabilities
Live V2 starts from team scoring intensity, scales it to the time remaining, conditions the final-score distribution on the current score, applies only bounded in-play event adjustments, and then shrinks the result toward margin-free live market probabilities. The same probability must reproduce fair odds, edge and expected value or the decision is blocked.
Why a WATCH signal may still say NO PLAY
Outcome probability is the model's probability of the selection. Decision confidence is an evidence score out of 100, not a probability. Live V2 remains shadow-only until prospective results demonstrate calibration, so WATCH can collect evidence without making an eligible bet.
