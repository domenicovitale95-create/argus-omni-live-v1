OilUnderstand what you are actually buying

Research selection · 4 September 2026

COMPLEX EXPOSURE · ETC

WisdomTree WTI Crude Oil Longer Dated

Tracks an index of oil futures contracts. It gives oil-market exposure without buying physical barrels.

Returns can differ substantially from the spot oil price. Replacing expiring contracts can help or hurt returns; collateral and swap risks also matter.

Official product and risk details ↗

Oil prices and oil companies are different

An energy stock or sector ETF owns businesses. An oil-futures ETC follows contracts. They can perform very differently.

This is a product to understand, not a default long-term portfolio foundation. No leveraged or short product is selected.
