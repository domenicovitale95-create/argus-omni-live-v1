How ARGUS builds the Daily Slip
The betting shortlist accepts only decisions already classified PRIME or VALUE by the Decision Scheduler. A Correlation Guard then removes concentrated selections from the same fixture, team or tightly related league/time cluster. ARGUS never adds a weaker pick just to complete a multi-bet.
Why WAIT is a valid result
A betting system that always produces a bet is forced to lower its standards. In ARGUS, zero eligible selections is a complete decision: the current evidence does not justify exposure.
