Preparing exact scoresARGUS is reading cached match data first.
Exact Score is a high-variance market. A 10–15% model probability can still be the most likely single score while remaining unlikely overall. Treat scenario ranking and betting value as two separate questions.
How ARGUS classifies exact scores
MODEL means the score comes from the calibrated distribution only. WATCH means the score probability and history quality are interesting. VALUE requires real Correct Score odds plus enough trained edge. Exact Score does not inherit PRIME automatically from another market.
