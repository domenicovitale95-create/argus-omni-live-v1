Why ARGUS separates holdout evidence from ROI
Historical result accuracy, Brier score, log loss and exact-score coverage can be evaluated from archived results. Historical betting ROI cannot be reconstructed honestly without genuine pre-kickoff market prices, so ARGUS does not fabricate ten-year odds.
What “no hindsight” means here
Fixtures are processed chronologically. Team state is updated only after each result. A prediction becomes eligible only after each team has enough prior matches, and the holdout section remains later in time than the training section.
