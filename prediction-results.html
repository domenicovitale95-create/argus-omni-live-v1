How to read these results
Forecast result measures whether the frozen selection matched reality. Betting result only counts when ARGUS actually produced an actionable verdict with stake > 0 and a valid recorded price. A NO BET forecast can therefore be wrong without being a lost bet.
Integrity rule
Prediction fields are frozen before kickoff. Settlement only adds the verified final outcome. Historical losses cannot be rewritten into wins, and missing results remain pending until verified.
