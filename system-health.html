How the overall verdict is decided
DEGRADED is reserved for core platform or security failures. ATTENTION means the platform is available but a decision-critical layer — such as Autopilot freshness — is not healthy enough to trust as current. LIMITED covers intentional constraints such as a temporary zero-quota pause. OPERATIONAL means the core platform is healthy and the current decision state is sufficiently fresh; it never means a bet is guaranteed to win.
